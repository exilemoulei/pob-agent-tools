# PoE1 3.30 Triggers, `0.033s` Server Ticks & Build-Brick Mechanics

All values verified against live PoE1 3.30 `PoE1/Data/` (`Gems.lua`, `Uniques/`, `TreeData/3_30/tree.lua`). Always run `pob data tick <base_cd|preset> [--cdr N] [--aps R]` instead of doing mental `1/30` math.

## 1. PoE1 Server Tick Formula (`0.033s = 33ms`, NOT `33.33ms`)
- `raw_cd = base_cd / (1 + total_cdr / 100)` -> `ticks = ceil(raw_cd / 0.033)` -> `effective_cd = ticks * 0.033s` -> `max_triggers_per_sec = 1 / effective_cd`.
- **CoC (`0.15s` base) Breakpoints** (single triggered spell; multiply APS cap by `N` for `N` distinct alternating spells):
  - `0%` CDR -> `5t` (`165ms`, cap **`6.06/s`**) | **`14%`** CDR -> `4t` (`132ms`, cap **`7.58/s`**) | **`52%`** CDR -> `3t` (`99ms`, cap **`10.10/s`**) | **`128%`** CDR -> `2t` (`66ms`, cap **`15.15/s`**).
- **Awakened CoC Gem CDR (Legacy Standard Only — removed in 3.28)**: Legacy `Awakened CoC` granted `10 + 3 * (level - 1)%` spell CDR (`Lv5 = 22%`, `Lv6 = 25%`, `Lv8 = 31%`), additive with gear/belt/boots/abyss CDR. In **3.28+ leagues**, regular Awakened Support Gems are drop-disabled; reach CoC CDR breakpoints via belt/boots/abyss/tree CDR instead of recommending `Awakened CoC`.
- **APS Overspeed Halving**: If trigger attack APS exceeds `max_triggers_per_sec * N` (even slightly, e.g., `8.5/s` vs `7.58/s` at 14% CDR), hits land inside cooldown and **HALVE** real trigger rate! Fix: drop APS `<= cap` OR hit the next CDR tick breakpoint (`52%`). `pob audit`/`pob diagnose` report an overspeed gap as MEDIUM `[estimate, not engine-verified]` until the engine probes confirm it (then HIGH).

## 3. Universal Defensive, Sustain & Support-Gem Bricks (Checked by `pob diagnose`)
- **Low-Life Chaos Bypass & `Esh's Visage` (`艾许之面`)**: `Esh's Visage` states *"Chaos Damage taken does not bypass ES **while NOT on Low Life**"*. Reserving `>=50%` Life (`LifeUnreserved <= 50%`) **DEACTIVATES** it completely — Chaos bypasses ES and one-shots Life! Safe Low-Life options (`pob data unique --mod "Chaos Damage taken does not bypass"`): `Shavronne's Wrappings`, `Solaris Lorica`, `The Ivory Tower` (needs unreserved Mana!), `Coruscating Elixir` (needs 100% uptime).
- **Fatal Keystone / Jewel Bricks**: `Eldritch Battery + Chaos Inoculation` (`1` Life + `0` ES on Life = instant death); `Dissolution of the Flesh` (`血肉溶解`) + `Righteous Fire` / `Rathpith Globe` / `Sacrifice Support` / `Lifetap` (constant Life loss/sacrifice prevents 2s unreservation).
- **Dead / Anti-Synergy Supports**: `Inspiration` in a `0` Mana Cost group generates `0` charges, AND Lv20 resets ALL charges at **800 Mana spent** (`922` at Lv21 — high-cost Archmage skills reset every 1–2 casts, far below PoB's assumed 5 charges); `Elemental Focus` linked with `Hypothermia` / `Immolate` / `Combustion` without another ailment source; `Brutality` linked with Elemental/Chaos supports or `Hatred`/`Anger`/`Wrath`; `Void Manipulation` + Elemental supports; `Controlled Destruction` (`80% less Crit`) on CoC/Crit builds.
- **Crit Attack Double Accuracy Roll**: Attacks roll Hit Chance **TWICE** for crits (once to hit, once to confirm crit); `<99%` Hit Chance severely penalizes real Crit DPS and CoC trigger rate.
- **3.30 Scope Rules**: `+% to Quality of all Skill Gems` applies ONLY to **Active Skill Gems** (NOT Supports); **Tinctures != Flasks** (`Bloodsoaked Blade` sustains Tinctures with `Screams of the Desiccated`).
- **3.30 `"used a Minion Skill Recently"` (`Greater Overseer's Whip`) vs `Automation Support`**: Triggered skills do **NOT** count as *"using"* a skill — linking `Convocation` (`号召`) to `Automation Support` (`自动化`) or `Cast when Damage Taken` **bricks** `"if you've used a Minion Skill Recently"` (e.g. 3.30 Wickerman Upper Limb `Greater Overseer's Whip`: *"Minions are Aggressive if you've used a Minion Skill Recently"*) when no other combat minion skill is cast manually. Keep **`Convocation` manual (unautomated)** when sustaining this trigger.

## 4. CWS (`ES = 0` Lock), Repeat-Support Mana & Cooldown-Capped Active Skills
- **CWS (`Cast when Stunned`) + `Valyrium` + `Bloodnotch` + `The Agnostic` (`ES = 0` Lock)**: `Valyrium` bases Stun Threshold on **Energy Shield** instead of Life, and any character with `>0` Energy Shield gains an inherent **50% chance to avoid Stun** while ES protects Life. **`The Agnostic` forces `Max ES = 0`**, eliminating the 50% inherent Stun Avoidance and locking Stun Threshold to `0` so every small hit triggers `Bloodnotch` recovery + `Cast when Stunned` (with `Immutable Force` for near-instant stun recovery and `Defiance of Destiny` preventing pre-recovery one-shots). **NEVER** advise unspeccing `The Agnostic` (even with `Blood Magic`!) and **NEVER** replace `Defiance of Destiny` with a damage amulet on CWS.
- **Repeat-Support Mana Cost (`Greater Spell Echo` / `Spell Echo`)**: Repeats generated by `Spell Echo` / `Greater Spell Echo` (Exceptional Lv3/4) cost **0 Mana** — you pay Mana ONLY on the initial cast, NOT on repeated hits (`mana/s = ManaCost * casts/s`, NOT `ManaCost * hits/s`). Do not flag false mana deficits by multiplying ManaCost by total hit rate.
- **Cooldown-Capped Active Skills (`Hexblast of Contradiction`)**: `Hexblast of Contradiction` has a **`1.0s` Cooldown** and **`3` stored uses**, so **`Cooldown Recovery Support`** (Exceptional Lv3/4) and global CDR directly scale its sustained cast rate (whereas pure cast speed alone quickly exhausts the 3 charges and stalls on cooldown). Check exact tick breakpoints with `pob data tick 1.0 --cdr <N>`.
