# Third-Party Notices

This repository includes derived data generated from open-source third-party projects.

## Awakened PoE Trade

The following bundled data files derive Path of Exile 1 English (`en`) and Traditional Chinese
(`cmn-Hant`) metadata from [SnosMe/awakened-poe-trade](https://github.com/SnosMe/awakened-poe-trade):

- `pob_cli/src/pob_cli/data/map_mods_3_30.json` (generated by `pob_cli/scripts/update_map_mods.py`):
  T16/T17 map modifier templates, Atlas area base names, and client header strings.
- `pob_cli/src/pob_cli/data/apt_item_drops.json.gz` (generated by `pob_cli/scripts/update_apt_data.py`):
  trimmed boss, invitation, fragment, and map drop tables.
- `pob_cli/src/pob_cli/data/apt_non_gear_items.json.gz` (generated by `pob_cli/scripts/update_apt_data.py`):
  trimmed non-gear currency, fragment, scarab, divination card, and map bilingual dictionary.
- `pob_cli/src/pob_cli/data/apt_trade_supplements.json.gz` (generated by `pob_cli/scripts/update_apt_data.py`):
  trimmed trade stat polarity (`better`) and Chinese stat template supplements.
